Android Loading Instructions
  1. Unzip or download your desired Trio files into your Android Download folder.
  2. Open RootlessJamesDSP or RootlessZachDSP.
  3. Under DDC: Toggle ON → Tap "Load DDC file" → Select the .vdc file.
  4. Under Convolver: Toggle ON → Tap "Select impulse response" → Select the .irs file.
  5. Under Liveprog: Toggle ON → Tap "Load custom script" → Select the .eel file.
  6. In App Settings, ensure host sample rate is locked to 48000 Hz.
Frequently Asked Questions
How do I use RootlessJamesDSP without root?

No root is needed. Grant the audio capture permission once via ADB (adb shell pm grant me.bmax.apatch android.permission.CAPTURE_AUDIO_OUTPUT) or wirelessly using Shizuku. Then lock the host sample rate to 48000 Hz in the app settings.

What is the exact loading order for the 3 files?

1) Load .vdc into DDC for parametric biquad target tuning. 2) Load .irs into Convolver with Preamp at 0.0 dB. 3) Load .eel into Liveprog for lookahead peak limiting and driver protection. Turn off other OEM equalizers.

Why is the host sample rate locked to 48 kHz?

Android's AudioFlinger mixer runs natively at 48000 Hz. Matching 48 kHz natively prevents asynchronous rate-conversion artifacts, phase smearing, and audio latency jitter.
